THE METHOD

Superposition, then collapse.

A qubit holds every answer until it is measured. We work the same way on purpose: nothing is decided early, several complete directions get built in parallel, and the choice comes at the end with the work in front of you.

01
Hold every state

The brief goes in as a set of possibilities, not a single spec. Nothing is ruled out while it is still cheap to try.

02
Build in parallel

Real directions get built, not sketched — AI doing the volume, a human deciding what is good.

03
Measure, then collapse

You see the versions side by side and pick. One becomes the release; the rest stay on file.

THE RESEARCH LANE

Pushing at the qubit end.

Quantum work is early and we say so. We prototype against simulators and design our systems so the parts that will one day run on real qubits are already separated out — the search, the optimisation, the sampling. When the hardware arrives the software will not have to be rewritten to meet it.

Until then superposition stays a way of working: hold the possibilities, measure late, ship one.
