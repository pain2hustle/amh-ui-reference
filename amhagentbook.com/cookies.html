Cookie Notice

A plain-language explanation of browser storage used across AMH and Agent Book.

Effective August 8, 2026
ON THIS PAGEEssential storageAMH Academy local storageService providersAnalytics and advertisingYour controls

Essential storage

We may use cookies, local storage or similar browser features to keep sessions working, remember interface choices, protect forms, maintain security and preserve user-requested settings.

AMH Academy local storage

The Academy uses the functional local-storage key amh-learn-ai-v1 to keep course progress on the current device. The separate key amh-academy-resume-token is written only when you check “Allow this browser to remember my resume token.” It contains the opaque eight-digit token, not your password. You can remove it with the Academy’s “Forget saved token” control or your browser settings. Removing it does not delete a server-side saved session; use the data request page for that request.

Service providers

Infrastructure and security providers may set essential identifiers needed to deliver traffic, prevent abuse and maintain sessions. Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical signals to distinguish people from automated abuse.

Analytics and advertising

As of this notice’s effective date, these sites are not designed around cross-site behavioral advertising. If analytics or cookie practices materially change, we will update this notice and any consent controls required by law.

Your controls

You can remove or block browser storage through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in, preferences, reminders or other features from working.
