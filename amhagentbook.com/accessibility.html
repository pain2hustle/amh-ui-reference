Our goal

We aim toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA practices, including keyboard access, readable structure, text alternatives, visible focus, sufficient contrast and responsive layouts. This is an ongoing objective, not a claim that every screen has completed a formal audit.

Using the site

You may zoom text with your browser, navigate links and controls by keyboard, and use common screen-reader landmarks. Motion is reduced when your device requests reduced motion.
