    Knowledge for rebuilding, kept for 4026.

    6,000 knowledge packages a community would need to rebuild after a long break: clean water, food, shelter, tools, energy, health, maths, records, trade, language and more. Each package says why it matters, what it should contain and what it depends on. In-depth build sheets, with history, how it works, parts diagrams and step-by-step builds, start with the telescope and the watch.

    How it was made

    AI research teams drafted the packages in three sets of 2,000, each with its reason, proposed contents, dependencies and a safety note. A package says what should exist, not that it has been tested in the field. The full lists live on Apiary: read how the packages work.
