FAQ

Good questions.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. Send your current site and AMH builds a private preview first. You only pay when you want the finished release.

How much does it cost?

Basic is $499 for a sharper rebuild of your current pages. Premium is $999 for more pages, better UI, and clearer pricing and packages.

Who actually designs it?

Austin, founder of Artificial Mind Hive, designs every site himself: the logo concept, the layouts and every custom detail, directed exactly. Design isn't handed to AI or a template. Nothing ships until it's been checked on a real phone.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. The new site goes live on the domain you already own.

Can you do apps or tools too?

Yes: quoting, scheduling, dashboards and assistants like WALO. Service Pricer and WALO were built here.

Do you use AI?

Yes, where it earns its place. We know the AI tools and optimize them for your business: assistants that answer leads, inboxes that sort themselves, quoting that runs itself. The design is different: every logo and layout is designed and directed by Austin.

What happens after launch?

Edits keep coming. Sold projects get a private review room to send changes and see previews.
