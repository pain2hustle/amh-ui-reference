✦ SmartRot episode lab

Good rot for small brains.

A dad-built counterpunch to junk scrolling: silly, educational and fun 20-35 second shorts about mindfulness and dealing with big feelings. Every one teaches a real tool. Breathing. Repair. Focus. Jealousy. Bedtime.
