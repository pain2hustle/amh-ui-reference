Accessibility Statement

Effective date: July 22, 2026 | Last updated: August 3, 2026

Accessibility goals and support options for AMH Site Revival.

AMH Site Revival is operated by Service Pricer LLC.

AMH Site Revival wants its websites and app surfaces to be usable by as many people as practical, including people using keyboards, screen readers, zoom, high-contrast settings, and mobile devices.

Our Goal

We aim to follow the spirit of WCAG 2.1 AA for public pages and core account workflows where practical for a small business product.

What We Work On

  • Readable text contrast and scalable layouts.
  • Keyboard focus states and logical navigation.
  • Labels for forms, buttons, and controls.
  • Alt text for meaningful images where appropriate.
  • Responsive pages that work on phone, tablet, and desktop.
  • Reduced-motion respect where practical for animation-heavy pages.

Known Limits

Some previews, experimental tools, generated demos, third-party embeds, videos, maps, charts, or canvas/3D features may not yet meet every accessibility goal. We keep improving those surfaces as they move from prototype to production.

Reasonable Accommodation

If you cannot access information or complete a workflow, contact us and we will provide a reasonable alternate path, such as email support, a manual report, a plain-text document, or help completing the action.

Contact

Accessibility requests can be sent to amh@amhsiterevival.com. Include the page URL, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology or browser you used if relevant.
